GETTING STARTED

This guide explains how to sign in, manage your session, and keep your
account secure.

1 Signing In

To sign in, open the login page and enter your email address and
password. If your account has multi-factor authentication (MFA)
enabled, you will be prompted for a 6-digit verification code from
your authenticator app after your password is accepted.

If you enter an incorrect password five times in a row, your account
will be temporarily locked for security reasons. Wait for the lockout
period to end before trying again, or contact support if you need
immediate access.

2 Changing Your Password

Once signed in, you can change your password at any time from Account
Settings, Security, Change Password. Enter your current password
followed by your new password. Your new password must be at least 12
characters and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters,
numbers, and symbols.

3 Forgot Your Password

If you forget your password, click the "Forgot password?" link on the
sign-in page. We will email you a secure recovery link that lets you
set a new password. The recovery link expires after a short period for
your protection.

4 Staying Signed In

Your session remains active while you use the application. If you are
inactive for an extended period, you may be asked to sign in again.
You can manually sign out at any time from the account menu.

5 Account Lockout

For your protection, repeated failed sign-in attempts will temporarily
lock your account. This is a normal security measure and does not mean
your account has been compromised.
